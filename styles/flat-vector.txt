# READY-MADE LOOK: FLAT VECTOR
#
# Clean, modern, corporate-friendly. Reads well small, on a phone, muted.
# The one to pick if you are not sure.
#
# To use it:  copy this file over style_block.txt, then change the four colours
#             on the palette line to your own brand colours and nothing else.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Flat vector illustration with no outlines: solid shapes of flat colour, gently
rounded corners, no gradients except a single soft one in the background, and
long soft-edged shadows falling to the lower right as though from one light high
on the left.

The palette is exactly four colours and all four appear in every shot: deep navy
blue, warm coral, pale sand, and off-white. Nothing else. Navy for the darkest
shapes, coral for the one thing the eye should go to first.

Camera straight on at eye level, static, no perspective and no depth of field:
everything is equally sharp. Generous empty space around the subject.

Composed for a tall vertical frame: the subject is close and large, filling the
frame from top to bottom, with only a narrow margin around it.

One single continuous image filling the whole frame: not a grid of panels, not a
comic strip, not a storyboard, no dividing lines, no repeated copies of the
subject, no before-and-after side by side.

No outlines, no black keylines, no stroke around any shape, no cross-hatching,
no visible brush or pencil texture. The surfaces are perfectly flat.

No text of any kind: no words, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions,
no signs, no writing on anything. This holds even where an object would normally
carry markings: no compass points, no dial faces, no gauge numerals, no scale
marks, no keyboard letters, no clock numbers. Leave those surfaces blank.

No photographic human hands, arms or bodies at any point. Objects move by
themselves.

No dialogue, no music, no on-screen text.
