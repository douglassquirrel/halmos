# READY-MADE LOOK: INK AND WATERCOLOUR
#
# Warm, handmade, unhurried. Good for anything that wants to feel personal
# rather than institutional. Forgiving of imperfection, which suits it.
#
# To use it:  copy this file over style_block.txt, then change the palette line
#             if you want a different set of washes.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hand-drawn illustration on heavy cream cartridge paper, the tooth and grain of
the paper showing faintly through every wash. Confident dip-pen ink
line, slightly uneven in weight, with watercolour washes laid inside and
sometimes just outside it, leaving darker wet edges where each wash dried.

The palette is four washes and all four appear in every shot: dusty sage green,
burnt sienna, muted indigo, and raw ochre, over the cream of the paper. The
washes are transparent: where two overlap you can see both.

Lit as though by soft daylight from a window to the left, flat and even, no
strong shadows. Camera looking straight down at the page, static, as if the
drawing were lying on a table.

Composed for a tall vertical frame: the subject is close and large, filling the
frame from top to bottom, with only a narrow margin around it.

One single continuous image filling the whole frame: not a grid of panels, not a
comic strip, not a storyboard, no dividing lines, no repeated copies of the
subject, no before-and-after side by side.

The drawing bleeds off all four edges of the frame. We are not looking at a sheet
of paper lying on a surface, we are inside the drawing: no border, no margin, no
mount, no deckled or torn edge, no visible edge of the paper anywhere, no frame,
no drop shadow behind a sheet, no second background behind the picture. No sketchbook spiral, no tape, no pins, no drawing board, no
paintbrush or pen visible: only the picture itself.

No text of any kind: no words, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions,
no signs, no handwriting, no writing on anything. This holds even where an object
would normally carry markings: no compass points, no dial faces, no gauge
numerals, no scale marks, no clock numbers. Leave those surfaces blank.

No photographic human hands, arms or bodies at any point. Objects move by
themselves.

No dialogue, no music, no on-screen text.
