# YOUR VISUAL STYLE
#
# Everything below the line is sent to the image and video models with EVERY
# shot, so every picture in your video looks like it belongs with the others.
# Replace it with whatever look you want. Lines starting with # are ignored.
#
# Read STYLE.md before editing — it explains what works and, more importantly,
# what to forbid. The last few lines here are prohibitions; keep them unless you
# have a reason not to. They exist because without them the models add things
# nobody asked for.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Describe your look here: the medium, the lighting, the palette, the camera.
Be specific about materials and colours. Say what it is made of and how it is lit.

Composed for a tall vertical frame: the subject is close and large, filling the
frame from top to bottom, with only a narrow margin around it.

One single continuous image filling the whole frame: not a grid of panels, not a
comic strip, not a storyboard, no dividing lines, no repeated copies of the
subject, no before-and-after side by side.

No text of any kind: no words, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions,
no signs, no writing on anything.

No photographic human hands, arms or bodies at any point. Objects move by
themselves.

No dialogue, no music, no on-screen text.
